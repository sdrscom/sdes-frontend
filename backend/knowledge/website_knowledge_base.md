## Commercial advantages
Lead with these when users ask about benefits, savings, or why SDRS.

- 0% VAT on services inside the Dammam Bonded Zone until final shipment clearance. VAT and customs duty stay suspended while goods remain in the zone, which improves cash flow.
- Deferred customs duty and partial clearance. Duty is paid when cargo is released, including from pre-built warehouses.
- More free storage days than other operators, which reduces demurrage. Do not state a number of free days; it is not published here.
- Exclusive on-site customs inspection at SDES premises, available only to SDES customers, plus a dedicated customs unit with full authority to facilitate procedures.
- Dedicated cargo X-ray scanning for faster clearance. On-site inspection and X-ray are described as instant and available 24/7.
- Credit facility available.
- Single payment window.
- E-government integration with customs for clearance and real-time tracking.
- Port charges are not applicable on goods destined to the zone.
- Re-export does not attract customs duty. Duty applies only when cargo is released to the local Saudi market.
- A buyer inside Saudi Arabia who holds a valid customs duty exemption certificate does not pay duty on goods bought from the zone, even through a mediator.
- Local or imported materials used for manufacturing inside the zone are not subject to customs duty.
- Allowable storage period: three years. Extended storage without accruing customs penalties is part of the zone offer.
- 100% foreign ownership of operations, with no local partner required.
- A branch inside the zone is not required in order to do business.
- Value-added work inside the zone: labeling, refill, stickering, assorting, palletization, shrink-wrapping, and pre-export customization for GCC or local markets.

### Pre-built warehouses
Ready-to-use warehouses for high-value cargo: pipes, machinery, oil and gas equipment, and spare parts. Customs duty is deferred until cargo release. Each warehouse includes water, 3-phase electricity, communications, fire fighting, an administrative office, a kitchen, and a toilet. Security is 24 hours. Leaseholders pay for water, electricity, and sewage connections at prevailing rates. Leaseholders may construct warehouses.

Sizes:
- Small, 360 m²: spare parts, quick access, cost effective
- Medium, 540 m²: machinery storage, assembly space, flexible layout
- Large, 900 m²: oil and gas equipment, heavy machinery, multiple zones
- Extra large, 1,200 m²: bulk storage, distribution hub, custom sections

### Built-to-suit warehouses
Custom designs for specific operations. Benefits stated on the site: customizable designs, expand without increasing fixed costs, suited to specialized storage workflows, designed with SDES experts.

## Services
Six divisions:

1. Container services: shuttling from terminal to bonded area, loading and offloading, cross stuffing, customs inspection, stripping.
2. General / steel cargo: shuttling from terminal to bonded area, handling, loading and offloading.
3. Vehicle services: VIN cross-check, odometer reading report, exterior damage check and reporting, stickering.
4. Freight services: re-export, import/export, in-transit, bonded-to-bonded shipments.
5. Labour and equipment: forklift with operator, skilled labor, available at reasonable rates on request.
6. Trade and distribution: palletization, shrink-wrapping, labeling, pre-export customization for GCC or local markets, final production steps inside the bonded zone so cargo reaches market faster and avoids off-site processing.

Zone operations run 24/7. On-site customs facilities are part of the service offer.

## Operating rules freight forwarders ask about
- A branch inside the zone is not required.
- "Free Zone" must be specified on the bill of lading or manifest only for containerized cargo. General cargo, machinery, and vehicles may enter the zone directly without prior nomination.
- Transit goods may be stored in the zone and moved by land to neighboring countries under transit rules.
- Bills of lading can be split.
- Cargo from local and international suppliers can be consolidated inside the zone.
- Labeling, refill, stickering, and assorting are allowed inside the zone.

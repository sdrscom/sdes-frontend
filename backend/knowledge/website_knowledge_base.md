## Commercial advantages
Use these when the user asks about benefits, savings, why SDRS, cash flow, or VAT.

- 0% VAT on services inside the Dammam Bonded Zone until final clearance. Duty and VAT stay suspended while goods remain in the zone.
- Deferred customs duty and partial clearance. Duty on pre-built warehouse cargo is paid when the cargo is released.
- More free storage days than other operators, which cuts demurrage. No day count is published. Do not invent one.
- On-site customs inspection at SDES premises, for SDES customers, plus a customs team with authority to process procedures. Described as instant and available 24/7.
- Dedicated cargo X-ray on site.
- Credit facility.
- Single payment window.
- E-government link with customs and real-time tracking updates.
- No port charges on goods destined to the zone.
- No customs duty on cargo kept for re-export. Duty applies when cargo is released to the local Saudi market.
- A Saudi buyer with a valid duty-exemption certificate does not pay duty on goods bought from the zone, including through a mediator.
- No customs duty on local or imported materials used for manufacturing inside the zone.
- Storage period: three years, without customs penalties for the extended stay described on the site.
- 100% foreign ownership, no local partner required.
- A branch inside the zone is not required to do business.
- Value-added work inside the zone: labeling, refill, stickering, assorting, palletization, shrink-wrapping, and pre-export customization for GCC or local markets.

## Services
Six divisions. Zone operations and on-site customs facilities run 24/7.

1. Container services: shuttle from terminal to bonded area, loading and offloading, cross stuffing, customs inspection, stripping.
2. General / steel cargo: shuttle from terminal to bonded area, handling, loading and offloading.
3. Vehicle services: VIN cross-check, odometer report, exterior damage check and report, stickering.
4. Freight: re-export, import/export, in-transit, bonded-to-bonded.
5. Labour and equipment: forklift with operator, skilled labor, at reasonable rates on request.
6. Trade and distribution: palletization, shrink-wrapping, labeling, pre-export customization, final production steps inside the zone.

Cargo types named on the home page: vehicles, industrial equipment, consumer goods, raw materials, energy solutions, specialized storage.

## FAQs
- A branch inside the zone is not required.
- "Free Zone" must appear on the bill of lading or manifest only for containerized cargo. General cargo, machinery, and vehicles may enter without prior nomination.
- Duty is not paid on cargo stored for re-export. It applies when cargo is released to the local Saudi market.
- Storage time allowed: three years.
- Transit goods may be stored and may move by land to neighboring countries under transit rules.
- A bill of lading can be split.
- Cargo from local and international suppliers can be consolidated in the zone.
- Labeling, refill, stickering, and assorting are allowed.
- Port charges do not apply to goods destined to the zone.
- Manufacturing materials, local or imported, are not charged customs duty inside the zone.
- A Saudi customer with a valid duty-exemption certificate does not pay duty on goods bought from the zone, even via a mediator.
- Customs personnel are in the zone and can process procedures.
- Extra administrative offices are not required. Warehouses include water, 3-phase power, communications, fire fighting, an office, a kitchen, and a toilet. Security is 24 hours.
- Leaseholders may construct warehouses.
- Water, electricity, and sewage connections are paid at prevailing rates.
- Labor and equipment are available at reasonable rates on request.
